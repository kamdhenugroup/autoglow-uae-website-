When you compare **front-package and full-body PPF in Dubai**, you are choosing how much of the car’s painted exterior to cover. A front package concentrates on selected forward-facing panels. Full-body PPF extends coverage to the other painted panels named in the quote.

There is no single coverage level that suits every car owner. Start with the panels you want covered, the vehicle’s condition and the film being offered. Then compare written quotes with the same scope.

*Cover image is an illustration of coverage options, not an AutoGlow customer installation.*

## What does a front PPF package cover?

AutoGlow’s [PPF service page](/services/paint-protection-film) lists the Front Package panels as:

- Bonnet
- Front bumper
- Front fenders
- Mirrors
- Headlights

This option focuses on the front-end areas specified in the package. Confirm whether the bonnet means a full panel or a partial section, and ask how adjoining edges and pieces are treated. Package details can differ, so use the installer’s itemized panel list as the source of truth.

## What does full-body PPF cover?

AutoGlow lists the Full Body Package as the front-package areas plus doors, skirts, roof, pillars, quarter panels and trunk. The service page describes this as coverage across every painted panel.

Ask the installer to confirm the panel list for your exact vehicle. A car may have additional painted pieces or non-painted trim, and the name “full body” alone does not tell you how each item will be handled.

AutoGlow’s Complete Protection Package adds interior protection to full-body exterior PPF. The listed interior areas include the console and trim, infotainment screens, door cups and edge guards. Ask for these items to be itemized if you are comparing that option.

## Quick comparison

| | Front Package | Full Body Package |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main scope | Bonnet, bumper, fenders, mirrors and headlights | Front-package panels plus doors, skirts, roof, pillars, quarter panels and trunk |
| Coverage focus | Selected front-end panels | Painted exterior panels listed by the installer |
| Useful question | Which parts of the bonnet and bumper are included? | Are all painted panels on this vehicle listed in the estimate? |
| Quote comparison | Confirm the exact front panel boundaries | Confirm the complete panel list and any exclusions |

Coverage labels are a starting point. The signed quote for your vehicle should define the actual scope.

## How to decide which coverage fits your priorities

### Choose a front package if you want a focused scope

A front package may fit if you prefer to protect selected panels first, want to keep the covered area limited, or want to compare a focused installation against broader coverage. It does not cover the rest of the painted body unless those panels are separately included.

### Consider full-body coverage if you want a consistent exterior scope

Full-body PPF covers more painted panels under the AutoGlow package description. Ask for the total panel list and review it against your car. If you are deciding between partial and full coverage, compare both estimates for the same film, finish, preparation and warranty terms.

### Add interior protection only if those surfaces matter to you

Interior accent protection is a separate part of AutoGlow’s Complete Protection Package. It is not the same as exterior full-body PPF. If screens, piano-black trim, door cups or edges are important, ask which specific interior pieces are included and how they will be protected.

## Four questions to ask before booking

1. **Which exact panels are included?** Request a written list for the selected package and the specific car.
2. **What film and finish will be installed?** Confirm the brand and product line, and request the applicable manufacturer documentation.
3. **What preparation is needed?** Ask the shop to inspect the paint and identify any correction or repair separately from the film installation.
4. **What does the warranty cover?** Read the manufacturer’s current terms for the product being quoted and confirm any registration requirements in writing.

## Compare coverage for your vehicle

Before choosing a package, decide which panels are priorities and ask an installer to inspect the car. A clear quote should show the exact film, finish, covered panels, preparation and warranty terms. That gives you a fair comparison and makes it easier to spot exclusions.
